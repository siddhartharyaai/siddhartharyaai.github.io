Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

CreatorAuro Drive Sync ("the app") is a private internal tool. It is used only by its operator to sync files between the operator's own Google Drive and the operator's own server.

Data the app accesses

With your permission through Google sign-in, the app accesses Google Drive files and basic account information, only as needed to sync files.

How data is used

Data is used only to provide the sync function. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with third parties.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and deletion

Synced files and access tokens are stored only on the operator's server. You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
